# Talk To Them AI

**Talk To Them AI** is a web-based educational platform that lets users have natural conversations with AI-powered versions of historical figures like Albert Einstein, Marie Curie, Isaac Newton, and Richard Feynman.

---

## What It Does

- Simulates one-on-one dialogue with famous scientists, using OpenAI’s language models
- Each character responds in their own tone, style, and personality
- Users can explore subjects like physics, chemistry, biology, and more through conversation
- Sessions are tracked to preserve chat history and context

---

## Who It’s For

- **Students** who want to better understand complex topics
- **Teachers** looking for an interactive learning tool
- **Self-learners** who prefer conversational, intuitive education
- **Developers** interested in AI-powered educational tools

---

## Why It Exists

Traditional learning tools are static and impersonal.  
Talk To Them AI provides a dynamic, human-like learning experience that helps users retain information and stay curious.
